## 1. Scaled Content Abuse do Google (risco existencial)

_Google's current spam policy (spam-policies doc, last updated 2026-05-15) does NOT prohibit AI content. "Scaled content abuse" is defined by PURPOSE and VALUE: "many pages generated for the primary purpose of manipulating search rankings and not helping users," and the very first example is "using generative AI tools... to generate many pages without adding value for users." The policy is explicitly production-method-neutral — it targets scaled low-value content "whether automation, humans or a combination are involved" (blog.google, March 2024). So the wikijuridica factory is not doomed by being AI-assisted; it is at risk only if pages are unoriginal, low-value, mechanically paraphrase official sources, or are dumped at scale without per-page value. The operative defenses Google names map directly onto mechanisms this repo already has or can build: a hard per-page value/originality gate (the existing <0.70 similarity + anti-template gates), visible provenance to official sources (source_registry.json), a rendered author byline with OAB identity from content/site.json (E-E-A-T "who"), an explicit AI-assistance-plus-editorial-review disclosure block (Google's "how" question), a people-first "why" (the lane-informativa / paid-intent rules), and a quality-ordered STAGED release rather than a 10k dump. The dominant site-level risk is NOT site reputation abuse (that is a third-party/parasite policy, largely N/A to a single-author first-party portal) but the helpful-content site-wide classifier baked into core ranking since March 2024: a weak tail of pages drags the whole domain down — which is exactly why "publicação zero até o gate completo" is the correct architecture, not just caution. Empirically, one 16-month experiment found 70.95% of AI pages on new domains got indexed within ~36 days but only 3% still ranked in the top 100 after three months, with YMYL finance/health domains indexing worst (9/100 and 14/100) — indexation is necessary-not-sufficient; sustained ranking needs authority, uniqueness, and E-E-A-T. There is no formal "sandbox," but Mueller confirms a low-signal period for new domains, so a small unimpeachable seed set published first (the 300 refined drafts), earning crawl trust, then throttled waves gated on Search Console feedback, is the legitimate acceleration path. Note also: Google does not support IndexNow, so Googlebot discovery must lean on correct XML sitemaps, internal linking, and Search Console URL Inspection._

**Top recomendações:**
- P0 — Release em coortes ranqueadas por qualidade, nunca 10k de uma vez: primeira coorte = top ~200-500 paginas por score composto; gate de avanco = indexacao e permanencia reais no Search Console da coorte anterior. Motivo real nao e 'volume = spam' (Google nega isso), e sim limitar o raio de dano do classificador site-wide, que avalia o corpus inteiro e pune todas as paginas pela cauda fraca.
- P0 — Corte numerico de cauda antes de todo release: excluir da publicacao o quartil mais fraco de cada coorte (bloqueado para reescrita) e transformar a auditoria global de similaridade em gate bloqueante por coorte — 'relatively high amounts of unhelpful content' derruba o site inteiro, entao a proporcao de conteudo fraco publicado e a metrica de risco existencial.
- P0 — Gate executavel anti-parafrase de fonte oficial: medir sobreposicao corpo vs texto da fonte registrada na proveniencia e reprovar transformacao mecanica (sinonimizacao/stitching sao exemplos nomeados de spam); fonte oficial permanece referencia, nunca corpo.
- P1 — Pacote E-E-A-T de autor em toda pagina via internal/render + content/site.json: byline visivel 'Rafael Toledo, advogado — OAB/RJ 227191' linkando a pagina de autor dedicada com credenciais e politica editorial; e o sinal mais pesado para YMYL juridico e o que faltava nas paginas que decairam no experimento de 16 meses.
